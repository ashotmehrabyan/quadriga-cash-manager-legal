Quadriga Cash Manager Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Quadriga Cash Manager (the “Application”) is an internal cash-flow planning tool used exclusively by Quadriga (the “Company”). It is not offered to outside customers or the general public.

Information processed

The Application may process supplier and customer records, bills, invoices, outstanding balances, accounting payment references, account identifiers, locally entered payment schedules and forecasts, and technical information required to authorize a QuickBooks Online connection.

QuickBooks Online access

The intended QuickBooks Online integration uses Intuit's authorization process to retrieve accounting information for internal planning and reconciliation. The integration is designed to be read-only: it does not initiate payments or intentionally create, edit, or delete QuickBooks transactions.

Use of information

Information is used to review receivables and obligations, plan payments, forecast cash flow, and reconcile internal schedules with accounting activity. QuickBooks-derived information is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and security

The Application is designed to operate on Company-controlled equipment with a local database. The Company is responsible for protecting devices, credentials, authorization tokens, backups, and access to financial information. No system is completely secure.

Sharing

Access is intended for authorized Company personnel. Information may be disclosed where required by law or for legitimate authorized business administration, subject to applicable safeguards.

Retention and deletion

Application data is retained as reasonably necessary for internal business purposes and applicable legal obligations. Authorized personnel may manage locally stored records according to the Application's technical capabilities and Company backup and retention procedures. Revoking QuickBooks access prevents further access using that authorization but does not automatically delete previously retrieved local data or backups.

Access and disconnection

Authorized personnel can revoke the Application's QuickBooks authorization through applicable Intuit account controls. The Company manages internal access to the Application.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the Application changes. Material revisions will be reflected in an updated effective date.
