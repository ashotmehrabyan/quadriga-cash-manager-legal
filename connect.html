Connect or Reconnect QuickBooks Online

Quadriga Cash Manager is an internal financial planning application available only to authorized Quadriga personnel. It runs locally on a company-controlled Windows computer.

To connect or reconnect QuickBooks Online, open Quadriga Cash Manager on the authorized computer, navigate to its QuickBooks integration section, and follow the on-screen authorization instructions when the production connector is available.

Current status: Production QuickBooks connection is under development. This webpage does not initiate an OAuth connection or access QuickBooks data.
