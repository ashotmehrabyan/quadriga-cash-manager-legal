Quadriga Cash Manager Legal

End-User License Agreement

Effective date: November 16, 2026

1. Purpose

Quadriga Cash Manager (the “Application”) is internal business software used by Quadriga (the “Company”) for cash-flow planning, payment scheduling, forecasting, and review of accounting information.

2. Authorized use

The Application is intended solely for personnel authorized by the Company and for legitimate Company business. It is not offered or licensed for public use, resale, or third-party distribution.

3. Permitted functions

Authorized users may review obligations and receivables, prepare payment schedules, forecast account balances, retrieve authorized QuickBooks Online records, and compare internal schedules with accounting activity.

4. QuickBooks Online integration

The intended integration accesses QuickBooks Online through Intuit's authorized authentication process and retrieves information in read-only mode. The Application does not initiate payments, transfer funds, or intentionally create, modify, or delete QuickBooks transactions. QuickBooks Online remains the accounting system of record.

5. Financial planning limitations

Forecasts, projected balances, and schedules are estimates and may differ from actual bank, credit card, or accounting balances. Users are responsible for verifying material decisions against appropriate records. The Application is not professional accounting, legal, financial, or tax advice.

6. Confidentiality and security

Users must protect Company financial information and credentials, comply with Company security requirements, and not disclose confidential data to unauthorized parties.

7. Ownership

The Application is maintained for Company internal purposes. This agreement grants no third-party ownership or distribution rights.

8. Availability

The Company may update, suspend, or discontinue the Application. Continuous availability and error-free calculations are not guaranteed.

9. Third-party services

QuickBooks Online is provided by Intuit and is subject to its applicable terms. Quadriga Cash Manager is not an Intuit product.

10. Termination

The Company may revoke an individual's authorization to use the Application at any time. Confidentiality duties continue after access ends.

11. Changes

This agreement may be revised as the Application evolves, with an updated effective date for material revisions.
