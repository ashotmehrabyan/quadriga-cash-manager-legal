Disconnect QuickBooks Online

Quadriga Cash Manager is an internal financial planning application for authorized Quadriga personnel.

To revoke the application's access to QuickBooks Online, an authorized QuickBooks administrator can review and disconnect the application from the connected-app management area of the company's QuickBooks account. When available, the local Cash Manager integration settings may also provide a disconnect control.

Important: This page provides instructions only; visiting it does not revoke access. Disconnecting QuickBooks does not necessarily erase information previously imported into the local Cash Manager database or its backups. Contact the administrator to request review or deletion of locally retained data.
